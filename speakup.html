SpeakUp

Build a language you can see.

SpeakUp turns language learning into a climb. Play focused games, practise real speech, pass a strict level test, and watch another floor rise in your personal language tower. Progress is not a streak here. It is something the learner earns and builds.

01

Tower progression

Every level leaves something standing.

The tower is not decoration. It is the learner's history, progress bar, and next challenge in one object.

A skyline made from real progress

Lessons prepare the floor. The exam makes it permanent.

Each language becomes its own building. Completed levels are solid and illuminated, the current floor stays under construction, and future levels remain visible as scaffolding. Milestone floors become anchors, so meaningful progress cannot disappear with a missed day.

Ping can explain course words in plain language, give a focused study tip, speak an example aloud, or suggest what to practise next. The assistant stays connected to the learner's chosen language and current course context.

05

Conversation practice

Speak before the real conversation.

Personas, live captions, optional translation, and a useful debrief turn speaking into practice rather than performance.

Live scenario · Café order

A calmer place to find the words.

Learners can call different personas, follow live captions, request a translation, and leave with corrections that flow back into the review queue. The current dialogue uses a scenario graph while the product remains a prototype.
